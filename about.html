About

Hi, I'm Rebecca — your Utah notary, day or night

I started SafeSeal Document Solutions to make notarization one less stressful thing on your to-do list — whether that's closing on a home, setting up a power of attorney for a parent, or getting a form witnessed before a deadline.

My approach

I started this business for people who struggle to reach a notary — because of a packed schedule, a disability, a hospital stay, or hours that don't fit a typical office. My goal is for every signer to feel like they're working with someone who understands what they're going through, not just someone stamping a form for a fee.

I show up on time, explain each step in plain language, and treat every document — and every client's privacy — with the seriousness it deserves.
