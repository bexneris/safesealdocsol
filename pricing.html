Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing

No surprise fees. Notary fees are set by state law; travel and convenience fees are added separately and confirmed before you book.

Service Starting Price Notes
General Notarial Acts $10 Utah's statutory maximum, per act — acknowledgments, jurats, oaths/affirmations, signature witnessing, certified copies
Estate & Family Documents $10 Per notarial act — power of attorney, wills, trusts, advance directives
Real Estate & Property $10 Per notarial act — deeds, title transfers, lease agreements
Remote Online Notarization (RON) $25 Utah's statutory maximum per act, once RON platform is connected
Loan Signing Services $100 Full purchase/refinance document package
Apostille Coordination $50 Notarization plus help preparing your submission for apostille
Bilingual Document Orientation & Guidance $45 A procedural walkthrough of your signing in Spanish or English
Mobile travel fee $25 + $1.50/mi $25 base covers the first 15 miles; $1.50 per mile beyond that, up to 100 miles
After-hours appointment +$50 Before 7am, after 11pm, or same-day rush
Notary fees (General Notarial Acts, Estate & Family Documents, Real Estate & Property, RON) are Utah's statutory maximums (Utah Code) — set at the legal cap, never above it. Loan Signing, Apostille Coordination, Bilingual Guidance, travel, and after-hours fees are service fees set by us, not capped by notary fee law.

A Notary Is Not an Attorney

English: A notary public is not an attorney and cannot give legal advice, explain the legal effect of a document, or help you choose which form to use. For legal questions, please consult a licensed attorney.

Español: Un notario público no es un abogado y no puede dar asesoría legal, explicar el efecto legal de un documento, ni ayudarle a elegir qué formulario usar. Para preguntas legales, consulte a un abogado con licencia.
