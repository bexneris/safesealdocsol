Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing

No surprise fees. Notary fees are set by state law; travel and convenience fees are added separately and confirmed before you book.

Service Starting Price Notes
Notarial act (acknowledgment, jurat, oath/affirmation, or signature witnessing) Up to $10 Utah's statutory maximum, per act
Certified copy Up to $10 Utah's statutory maximum, per page certified
Mobile travel fee $25 + $1.50/mi $25 base covers the first 15 miles; $1.50 per mile beyond that, up to 100 miles
Loan signing package $150 Full purchase/refinance document set
Remote online notarization (RON) Up to $25 Utah's statutory maximum per act, once RON platform is connected
After-hours appointment +$50 Before 7am, after 11pm, or same-day rush
Additional signer / document +$[XX] Per extra signer beyond the first
Notary fees shown above are Utah's statutory maximums (Utah Code) — you're free to charge less, but not more. Travel, loan-signing, and after-hours fees are set by you based on typical industry rates; adjust them anytime as you learn what works in your market. The additional signer/document fee is still a placeholder — let us know what to set it at.
